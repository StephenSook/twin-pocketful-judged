    How we computed this

    • Handoffs are text messages from a seat that @mention another participant.
    • Verdicts are the words ACCEPT or REJECT followed by a revision hash, as the mandates require.
    • Rejections followed by a seat commit count REJECT verdicts that a later seat commit followed. Open the room message to see what the rejection asked for, then the commit to see what changed.
    • Human messages after dispatch counts messages from any human after the first one. Zero means the run was hands off.
    • Retried or undelivered messages come from BAND's own per-recipient delivery records inside the export, as of the moment it was downloaded.
